The program

A science-practice model

Graduate training in our program is based on a science-practice model, where students are trained both as researchers as well as applied scientists equipped to work with organizations on human resource issues.

The current faculty have a wide range of research interests, many of which center around the psychological experiences of people at work. Students are strongly encouraged to participate in faculty research as they transition to their own individual research projects.

Read more about the program →

Please note that this program is part of Purdue University's Department of Psychological Sciences (West Lafayette) and is not affiliated with the Purdue Global I-O Psychology program. We offer a Ph.D. program in I-O Psychology and do not offer a terminal master's degree.
