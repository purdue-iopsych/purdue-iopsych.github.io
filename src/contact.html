Applying to the program

To ask whether someone is admitting a student, or about their lab and research, write to them directly. Prospective applicants are encouraged to do this — it is how fit gets established.

Where we are

Department of Psychological Sciences

The I-O psychology program is part of the Department of Psychological Sciences, on Purdue's West Lafayette campus.

Purdue University
Department of Psychological Sciences

West Lafayette is about an hour from Indianapolis and about two hours from Chicago. The closest international airports are Indianapolis International and O'Hare. There is more about the campus and the area on our program page.

This program is part of Purdue University's Department of Psychological Sciences in West Lafayette and is not affiliated with the Purdue Global I-O psychology program. If you meant to reach Purdue Global, none of the addresses on this page will get you there.
